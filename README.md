# Talbits

LNbits extensions and exploratory features by Tal.

A staging area for my LNbits work: extensions, exploratory features, and
experiments. Some of it is headed upstream to the LNbits project, some stays
here.

## What's here

- **Extensions**: standalone LNbits extensions at various stages of maturity.
- **Exploratory features**: forks and branches prototyping changes to LNbits itself.
- **Experiments**: ideas that may not survive contact with reality.

## Status

Nothing here is reviewed, endorsed or supported by the LNbits project. Some
repositories are prototypes: they may change, stall, or be abandoned without
notice.

Read the source, test before production use, and use them at your own risk.

## Upstream

Projects that mature here may be proposed to LNbits as pull requests. Don't
assume a repository is upstream-bound. Check its README for status.
